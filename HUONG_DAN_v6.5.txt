RENEW ZONE v6.5 - Sua Chi tiet nhan vien

1. Giai nen ZIP.
2. Tren GitHub thay public/index.html bang file trong ZIP.
3. Commit, cho Render Live va tai lai trang bang Ctrl+Shift+R.
4. Vao Chi tiet nhan vien: tu dong chon nhan vien dau tien, hoac chon nhan vien khac; chon khoang ngay va bam Cap nhat.

Ban nay sua luong hien thi phia giao dien: tai danh sach, tu dong chon nhan vien, hien trang thai va loi, khong can tai dashboard tong quan truoc.
Du lieu van lay tu cac API /api/dashboard, /api/accessory-sales va /api/my-commission, nguon don nhan vien nhap. Khong thay doi co so du lieu, chinh sach luong hay server.js.
Chua kiem thu voi co so du lieu Neon tren Render. Neu van loi, chup dong thong bao tai Chi tiet nhan vien hoac log Render.
